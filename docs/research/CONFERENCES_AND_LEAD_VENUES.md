# Conferences & other places leads can come from

Practical venues for a **mining / oil & gas capital intermediary** (issuers needing capital + desks that write checks).  
Conferences are for relationship density; daily EDGAR/SEDAR+ catches public filings between events.

**If dad only picks a few:** PDAC → NAPE → AME Roundup or Mining Indaba (by geography).

## B) Other high-value lead venues (not just conferences)

### 1. Public filings & news (already in toolkit)

| Source | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **EDGAR** (`src.edgar_watcher`) | US financing / distress / stage language | Daily scan |
| **SEDAR+** | Canadian juniors (PPMs, PEA/PFS/DFS, streams) | Weekly manual |
| Press / IR (Newsfile, Business Wire, company sites) | Closed financings → reverse-engineer providers | Weekly skim |
| Junior Mining Network / mining wires | PPM noise, but early alerts | Watchlist |

### 2. Capital-provider side (who writes checks)

- Provider websites + criteria pages (seed list: `data/seed_providers.json`)
- Royalty / streaming IR and deal announcements (Franco-Nevada, Wheaton, OR Royalties, Triple Flag, etc.)
- Bank energy desks (RBL / project finance) — better post-production / large tickets
- PE / private credit mining & energy pages (Denham, Orion, Appian, Breakwall, etc.)

Use closings in press to **map who actually funded whom**, then ask for intros on similar-stage deals.

### 3. Relationship / referral channels (still the best deals)

- Existing broker / landman / geologist / engineer network
- Law firms that do mining & O&G financings (they see mandates early)
- Accounting / audit shops for juniors and E&Ps
- Royalty companies and offtakers who decline a deal but will refer
- Prior clients who raise again at the next stage (PEA → PFS → construction)

### 4. Industry associations & local rooms

| Venue type | Examples | Lead style |
|---|---|---|
| Mining associations | PDAC membership events, provincial mining associations | Issuer intros |
| Petroleum associations | IPAA, SPE local chapters, state oil & gas associations | Operator / capital mix |
| Land / A&D | AAPL meetings, regional NAPE Prospect Expos | Farm-outs, acreage, partners |
| Chamber / energy councils | Houston, Calgary, Denver, Midland | Soft intros, local operators |

### 5. Online / always-on (lower quality, still useful)

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Company IR / “seeking capital” pages | Direct outbound |
| LinkedIn (energy PE, mining finance, RBL bankers) | Soft outreach after a filing hit |
| X / industry posters | Trend + who is active (see staged-capital research doc) |
| SEDAR+/EDGAR watchlists on specific tickers | Follow after first meeting |

Treat online as **triage**, not proof a company is legit — verify on EDGAR/SEDAR+/technical reports.

## C) How to work a conference for intermediary dealflow

1. **Go with a clear mandate book** — 2–5 live deals with stage, ask type, ticket size, geography.
2. **Split time** — half issuer/sponsor meetings, half capital desks (banks, PE, royalty, private credit).
3. **Ask capital desks one question** — “Do you take intermediary intros, and on what fee basis?” Log `broker_friendly` only when confirmed in writing.
4. **Prefer stage fit** — pre-FID ≠ First Citizens RBL; producing / project debt ≠ early PPM equity.
5. **Follow up in 48 hours** with a one-pager + filing links; put everyone in Opportunities / Providers / Interactions.

Compliance reminder: success fees on **securities** placements can require BD/CAB registration. Commercial loan intros ≠ equity placement. Get counsel before % fees on securities.
